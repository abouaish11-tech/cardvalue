## Owner decisions

1. **The Amex Blue Cash offers keep moving.** Last week they dropped to $250 and $150. Today the pages show $300 and $200 again, with no limited-time label or end date. I applied today's values. If they keep moving week to week, consider showing an "offers vary" note for these two cards.
2. **The Citi AAdvantage bonuses fell a lot.** The visible page text leaves the amount blank. The new values come from Citi's own pricing feed (default offer) that fills it in. That feed matched every other Citi card checked this week. The earlier 80k and 125k offers were probably limited-time offers that have ended. Worth a quick look in a normal browser.
3. **Some Amex Delta credits are still missing from the pages, now for a second week.** These Delta Stays credits don't appear: SkyMiles Platinum ($150) and SkyMiles Reserve ($200). The $120 Rideshare credit on SkyMiles Reserve doesn't appear either. All of them were left in the data. Reserve Business still shows its "Up to $250 Delta Stays Credit", and Gold and Gold Business still show theirs. Consider removing the missing ones.
4. **The Amex Platinum $300 Equinox credit is still missing from the page, now for a third week.** "Equinox" no longer appears anywhere on the card page. It was left in the data. I recommend removing it.
5. **Upcoming and small gaps.** IHG Premier's 5x on general travel ends for purchases after 12/31/2026, so check its travel rate in January. Robinhood Gold's $50/yr membership cost couldn't be confirmed, because the page shows only $5/month. Rakuten Amex's welcome offer isn't stated on any page that loaded. Several pages list credits we don't track: Global Entry/TSA credits, the JetBlue Premier companion credits, and Apple TV/Music on Sapphire Reserve.
