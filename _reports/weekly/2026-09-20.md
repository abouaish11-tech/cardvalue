## Top 3 suggestions

### 1. "Can you hit the bonus?" check under every sign-up bonus — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 8 (sign-up-bonus pitfalls: fees and refunds don't count, missed windows; MyFICO/FlyerTalk threads linked below). Buzz: this is a bonus-chasing week (Delta up to 80K/90K through Nov 4, Hilton Surpass 130K + Free Night from Sept 10, Marriott Boundless 125K ends Sept 30, Ink Cash and Southwest elevated offers ended Sept 17). The drawer already printed "after spending $5,000 in 3 months" with no relation to the visitor's own spend.
- **What:** In the detail drawer, compare total monthly spend (the same five inputs) with `spendRequired / months`. Green: "you'd reach $X in about N months, inside the window." Amber: "you'd be $Y short, you'd need about $Z/month more." Footnote: fees, refunds and balance transfers don't count.
- **Impact:** trust (the site tells the visitor when a headline bonus is not for them). **Must not touch:** ranking maths, wallet optimizer, paywall. It doesn't.

### 2. Break-even line on annual-fee cards (S)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 1 is the biggest (about 15 of 45 threads): "is the fee still worth it" after CSR $795 / Platinum $895 / Gold $325 renewals. MyFICO "Keep or Close your Chase Sapphire Reserve" thread: "tough to justify the high annual fee and the hassle of keeping track of all the monthly credits" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Are-you-planning-to-Keep-or-Close-your-Chase-Sapphire-Reserve/td-p/6835638). FlyerTalk cites an analyst estimate that CSR cardholders fell 25%+ via downgrades/cancellations (https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/chase-ultimate-rewards/2206039-sapphire-reserve-transition-existing-cardholders-26-oct-2025-a.html).
- **What:** In the "Value vs. Cost" box, when net value is negative: "At your spending this card loses $X/yr. You'd need about $Y/month more in [best category] to break even." Y = deficit / (12 × multiplier × point value). Display only.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust; makes the net-value number actionable. **Must not touch:** ranking, paywall.

### 3. "Where do you buy groceries?" sub-question and cap warnings (M)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 4: Walmart/Target/Costco are not "groceries" on most cards, and caps ($6,000 on Blue Cash Preferred, $500/month on Custom Cash) bite. Bankrate notes BCP's grocery cap hasn't changed since 2013 (https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/cash-back/issuers-need-to-update-spending-caps/); MyFICO threads on Walmart coding (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Does-AAA-Visa-count-Walmart-as-grocery-Any-other-5-cards/td-p/6849122).
- **What:** One optional toggle under Groceries ("mostly supermarkets" vs "mostly Walmart/Target/Costco"); when superstore, route grocery spend at 1x on cards that exclude superstores (needs a per-card flag in `cards.json`), and show an inline "cap hit" note in the drawer when monthly spend exceeds a cap.
- **Effort:** M (data flag per card + input + calc change, so it touches the ranking inputs and needs owner sign-off). **Impact:** accuracy for the largest spending category. **Must not touch:** paywall, wallet maths beyond consuming the new multiplier.

## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Research was search-snippet only (Reddit and all direct thread fetches blocked). Rough frequencies are out of about 45 forum threads surfaced.

1. **"Is the fee still worth it?"** (~15/45). MyFICO CSR keep-or-close: "more of a responsibility now to make sure I get my moneys worth" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Are-you-planning-to-Keep-or-Close-your-Chase-Sapphire-Reserve/td-p/6835638); Bogleheads "One more year of Amex Platinum" (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=463251). → Break-even line (suggestion #2); anniversary-month input that re-ranks with next year's fee/credit changes.
2. **Coupon-book credit fatigue** (~10/45). FlyerTalk Platinum refresh thread, users "weary of the coupon-book" (https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/american-express-membership-rewards/2203601-amex-usa-personal-platinum-refresh-895-fee-september-2025-a-3.html); Thrifty Traveler: "you'll practically need a spreadsheet" (https://thriftytraveler.com/news/credit-card/amex-statement-credits-are-out-of-hand/). → Per-card "credits I'd actually use" checklist that re-computes net value; the site's realistic-credit discount already addresses half of this. Copy: rename "usable credits" to "credits you'll realistically use".
3. **Not knowing what points are worth / devaluations** (~8/45). MyFICO on Chase "Points Boost": "essentially nerfing the redemption" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Chase-Sapphire-Preferred-new-Points-Boost/td-p/6829440); Bogleheads on Chase Travel pricing vs Expedia (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=474680). → Per-card point-value slider (cash-out floor / editorial / aspirational) with tooltip explaining the 1.5¢ choice; a Hyatt 4:3 note for CSP/Ink Preferred from Oct 1.
4. **Category coding and caps** (~6/45). Walmart/Target/Costco not groceries; BCP $6,000 cap unchanged since 2013 (https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/cash-back/issuers-need-to-update-spending-caps/). → Suggestion #3.
5. **Which card for what / too many cards** (~8/45). Bogleheads "rewards vs hassle factor" (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=408767). → In the wallet optimizer show the 1- vs 2- vs 3-card delta and a "simplest wallet within $50 of the best" option.
6. **Downgrade vs cancel, score fear** (~6/45). MyFICO "Will closing my oldest open card REALLY hurt score" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Will-closing-my-oldest-open-card-REALLY-hurt-score-or-not/td-p/6840521). → "Downgrade path" line on fee cards (needs per-card data).
7. **Application rules and denials** (~7/45). Chase "too soon" denials (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Card-Applications/Chase-Too-many-request-for-credit-or-opened-accounts-with-us/td-p/6854824); Bonvoy 24-month account denial (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Chase-Bonvoy-Eligibility/td-p/6863785). → Optional "cards opened in last 24 months" input that flags Chase cards at 5+; eligibility note per card.
8. **Sign-up-bonus pitfalls** (~5/45). Fees don't count toward spend (https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/american-express-membership-rewards/2083377-amex-statement-credits-minimum-spend-requirement.html); Amex "not eligible" pop-up (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Card-Applications/Amex-not-eligible-for-welcome-bonus-even-though-I-should-be/td-p/6715411). → Built this week (suggestion #1).
