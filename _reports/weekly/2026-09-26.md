## Top 3 suggestions

### 1. "Keep, downgrade, or cancel?" verdict on every annual-fee card — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 1 is again the largest (15+ distinct threads/articles this month: Bogleheads "Chase Sapphire Reserve fee increase" at 7+ pages, MyFICO keep-or-close and "Moving on from Chase Sapphire Reserve", Quora "cancel or downgrade to avoid the $795", Mighty Travels "CSR fee: $700 gap vs downgrade to Preferred" dated Sept 2026). The buzz list adds fuel: Sapphire Reserve is the most-discussed card (10th-anniversary credits vs "$795 coupon book" downgrade pieces), Sapphire Preferred loses its 10% anniversary bonus and 1:1 Hyatt transfers on Oct 1, Citi is publishing "why I'm downgrading Strata Elite to Premier". Last week's unbuilt suggestion #2 (break-even line) is folded in; this is the same need with a week more evidence and a sharper product answer (name the downgrade target, show the gap).
- **What:** In the detail drawer, under "Value vs. Cost", a box that reads Keep it / Downgrade to X / Cancel or downgrade, with this card's net value vs the net value of the no-fee or lower-fee card in the same family it can be product-changed to, the dollar gap, and (when net is negative) how much more monthly spend in its best category would break even. A footnote says the sign-up bonus is excluded (renewal question), most issuers refund a fee if closed within ~30 days, and to confirm the product change with the issuer.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust and time-on-page: the site now answers the question people actually type into forums, and the answer is personalised. **Must not touch:** ranking maths, wallet optimizer, paywall, checkout. It doesn't: display-only, reads `calcNetValue`.

### 2. "I book travel: direct / through the card's portal" toggle with a portal price premium (M)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 4 (5–6 threads, growing since the Reserve refresh moved value into Chase Travel): Bogleheads "8x on Chase Travel, is it worth it?" reports an IHG night at $330 on Expedia vs $439 on Chase Travel (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=474680); TPG's portal comparison puts Chase Travel 5.94% above direct (https://thepointsguy.com/guide/credit-card-travel-portal-battle). Our data already carries portal-only rates in card notes ("5x via Chase Travel") while `rewards.travel` uses the direct rate, so the model is half-built.
- **What:** One toggle under the Travel input (default: direct). Portal mode applies each card's portal multiplier (new optional `portalTravel` rate per card) minus a configurable price premium (default 5%, cited), and the drawer's travel row says "8x only on Chase Travel; 4x direct". New this week; not in a prior report.
- **Effort:** M (per-card data field, a spending input, a calc change). **Impact:** accuracy for premium travel cards; addresses the "headline rate is illusory" complaint directly. **Must not touch:** paywall; needs owner sign-off because it changes ranking inputs.

### 3. "Cards opened in the last 24 months" input that flags Chase cards (S)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 7 (6+ threads): MyFICO "Chase - Too many request for credit or opened accounts with us" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Card-Applications/Chase-Too-many-request-for-credit-or-opened-accounts-with-us/td-p/6854824), "Chase 5/24 Rule and the proper denial reasons"; FinanceBuzz notes AU cards and closed cards still count. Last week's report mentioned this only in the pain section, never as a top-3; it is promoted because six of our top-ten net-value cards at default spending are Chase-issued, so a 5/24 visitor is being shown cards they cannot get.
- **What:** A single optional number input (0–9) in the personalize drawer. If ≥5, Chase rows get a "Likely denied (5/24)" badge and a one-line drawer note; the wallet optimizer output gets a warning if it names a Chase card. Display-only: no change to ordering or maths.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust (the site stops recommending unobtainable cards) and fewer wasted apply clicks. **Must not touch:** ranking order, paywall, wallet maths.

Runner-up (M, new): a per-credit "will you use it?" checklist in the drawer for coupon-style credits (pain theme 2, 10+ sources: "you'll practically need a spreadsheet"). Held back because it would re-score credits and therefore touch the ranking. Last week's #3 (superstore grocery toggle and cap warnings, M) still stands.

## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Search-snippet research (~42 queries); Reddit is not reachable through search or fetch, and every direct forum fetch was blocked. Dates are as shown in snippets or URLs; several MyFICO threads appear to be late 2025 to spring 2026 rather than the last 30 days.

1. **"Is the fee still worth it?" — keep, downgrade or cancel** (15+ sources; largest). "There isn't enough differentiation between the Reserve and the Preferred to justify a $700 difference unless you are a really big spender" (Bogleheads/MyFICO downgrade threads, https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=454993). → **Built this week** (verdict box).
2. **Coupon-book credits** (10+ sources). "most of those credits arrive in monthly and quarterly slivers engineered to expire if you forget about them" (https://onemileatatime.com/insights/american-express-platinum-card-worth-it/); "Amex advertises over $3,000 in statement credits, most people will realistically see $1,500 to $2,200" (https://cardstack.money/articles/credit-card-reviews/amex-platinum-review-2026). → Per-credit "will you use it?" checklist in the drawer that re-scores credits, plus a "hassle" count next to the fee (M, runner-up above).
3. **Caps, "up to" rates and merchant coding** (8+). "Many families hit the $6,000 annual limit around the middle of the year, and the threshold hasn't increased since the card debuted" (https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/cash-back/issuers-need-to-update-spending-caps/); Walmart/Target/Costco "earn only 1x" on Amex Gold (https://mycarddeals.com/offers/amex-gold-grocery/, Sept 2026). → "Cap hit in month N, blended rate X%" line in the drawer; superstore grocery chip (last week's #3).
4. **Portal-only multipliers and portal price premiums** (5–6). Bogleheads: "$330/night at Expedia … $439 on Chase Travel" (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=474680); TPG: Chase Travel 5.94% dearer than direct (https://thepointsguy.com/guide/credit-card-travel-portal-battle). → Suggestion #2.
5. **Not knowing what points are worth; silent devaluations** (8+). "Using them for cash back is just giving away a free 50% of their value if you have a Sapphire Reserve" (https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=445818); Capital One Emirates 1:0.75 (https://www.cnbc.com/select/credit-card-devaluations-how-to-protect-rewards/). → Show the assumed ¢/pt inline next to every points-card value with a "change" link to the existing Cash/Transfer toggle; add a "transfer partners (~2¢)" third mode.
6. **Sign-up bonus mechanics** (8+). "if you need to spend $4,000 in 90 days but do it in 92 days, you're probably not going to receive the bonus" (https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/5-reasons-a-credit-card-company-wont-pay-out-your-sign-up-bonus); a TPG staffer missed 60,000 AA miles "by less than $50". → Extend the (unmerged) bonus check with a "tight (<10% margin)" state and the "fees, refunds, balance transfers don't count" footnote.
7. **Denials and 5/24** (6+). MyFICO "Chase - Too many request for credit or opened accounts with us" (https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Card-Applications/Chase-Too-many-request-for-credit-or-opened-accounts-with-us/td-p/6854824); "Closed cards still count if they were opened within the past 24 months" (https://financebuzz.com/chase-524-rule-explained). → Suggestion #3.
8. **Too many cards / simplifying; downgrade vs cancel** (8+). MyFICO "Downsizing my credit card portfolio" (March 2026, https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Downsizing-my-credit-card-portfolio/m-p/6860103); AOL/Bankrate "With 7 cards, the credit card game is getting boring" (https://aol.com/7-cards-credit-card-game-180411088.html); Chase "One Sapphire" rule. → Wallet builder: show the marginal value of each added card ("3rd card adds only +$38/yr"); "cards I already have" multi-select flagging overlap.

Minor: foreign transaction fees noticed after a trip (https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/foreign-transaction-fees/) → "I travel abroad" checkbox applying a 3% penalty to FTF cards; "what counts as travel" (29-page FlyerTalk thread) → tooltip on the Travel input; carrying a balance → one-line banner that rewards don't matter if you carry a balance.
