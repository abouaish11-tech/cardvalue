- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust; stops the free preview's first clickable card from sending people to a dead application. **Must not touch:** ranking order, wallet maths, paywall, checkout. It doesn't.

### 2. A one-line "effort cost" for statement credits (S)
- **Why now:** Pain theme 1 and 2 are the fee-creep and coupon-book complaints, and this week's evidence is about *effort*, not face value: Fortune on the new fees, "more benefits means a labyrinth of monthly statement credits, opt-in offers, and spending caps that take effort to use fully" ([Fortune, Mar 30 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/03/30/credit-card-annual-fees-soaring-why-people-keep-paying-them-perks-robinhood-american-express-chase/)); the Sapphire Reserve DoorDash change forces an order of use, "You are effectively forced to use the $15 credit first, and then the $10 credits become available" ([Doctor of Credit, updated Oct 1 and 5](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-adding-doordash-15-monthly-credit-which-can-be-used-for-anything/)) and "The non-restaurant promos are hard to use without additional spending" ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/news/chase-sapphire-reserve-doordash-credit-increase)); Lululemon gift cards stopped triggering the Platinum's $75 quarterly credit around Oct 5 ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/reports-lululemon-gift-cards-no-longer-trigger-american-express-platinum-statement-crediy/)); a FlyerTalk cardholder tallying "$300 dining credit, $250 EDIT, $180 apple music & TV, $300 chase lounge/priority pass… $1030 total" ([FlyerTalk](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/chase-ultimate-rewards/2209859-chase-sapphire-reserve-2025-benefit-tally.html)). ### 3. "If you cancel or downgrade, what happens to your points" line in the keep/downgrade box (S)
- **Why now:** Amex stopped automatically pooling Membership Rewards for new cards on about Oct 15: new cards get a separate MR account unless linked under "Manage Connections", and closing an unlinked card forfeits its points ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/american-express-no-longer-automatically-pools-membership-rewards-points-from-new-cards/), [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/news/amex-stops-automatically-pooling-membership-rewards-points-new-cards/), [View from the Wing, "check this before you cancel a card"](https://viewfromthewing.com/amex-stops-automatically-pooling-new-card-points-check-this-before-you-cancel-a-card/)). Pain theme 6 (downgrade vs cancel) has the same risk from the visitor's side: "You will immediately forfeit any unredeemed Membership Rewards points, unless you keep another American Express card or checking account that can hold them"; Citi ThankYou points "must be used within 60 days after closing" ([WTOP, May 2026](https://wtop.com/news/2026/05/what-happens-to-your-credit-card-rewards-when-you-close-an-account/)); Chase "typically won't refund you for your card's annual fee unless you cancel within 30 days of it posting" ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/guide/cancel-or-downgrade-credit-card)); and FlyerTalk's retention thread has March 2026 data points of "Got shot down with my Sapphire Reserve" and "No retention offer" ([FlyerTalk](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/chase-ultimate-rewards/2147554-chase-retention-offers-2024-26-a-5.html)). Our keep/downgrade verdict already names the target card and says to confirm with the issuer; it says nothing about the points. New this week.
- **What:** A small `CANCEL_NOTES` table keyed by `currency` (chase_ur, amex_mr, citi_ty, capital_one_miles, bilt_points, plus a generic co-brand line "miles live in your airline/hotel account and are safe") rendered as one footnote line under the verdict when the verdict is Downgrade or Cancel, plus the Amex "new cards are not pooled since Oct 2026, link them first" sentence for `amex_mr`. The wording for each issuer should be confirmed by the owner against the issuer's terms before shipping; the sources above cover Amex, Citi and Chase.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust; prevents the one mistake that turns a sensible downgrade into a loss of a sign-up bonus' worth of points. **Must not touch:** the verdict maths, the downgrade-path table, ranking, paywall.

Method: Playwright 1.56 with the pre-installed Chromium against `python3 server.py` on `localhost:3456`. `main` (`eb191de`, the commit GitHub Pages run #49 deployed on 2026-10-13) was audited first: **95 PASS, 0 FAIL, 1 COULD-NOT-TEST**. Widths 1280×800 and 375×812 (mobile emulation with touch). ## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Source: the "pain" sub-agent (64 searches, 58 with results; snippets only). Reddit was entirely unreachable (the search tool refuses `reddit.com`), so Reddit evidence is second-hand via outlets that quote threads; Bogleheads, FlyerTalk and app-store pages could not be fetched, so quotes are as they appeared in snippets and most are undated or older than 30 days, marked where known. Ranked by apparent frequency.

1. **"Is the fee worth it?" is now a fee-creep and coupon-book-effort grievance** (very common). "more benefits means a labyrinth of monthly statement credits, opt-in offers, and spending caps that take effort to use fully" ([Fortune, Mar 30 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/03/30/credit-card-annual-fees-soaring-why-people-keep-paying-them-perks-robinhood-american-express-chase/)); "the higher fees announced in 2025 could lead to a wave of cancellations and downgrades in 2026 as existing cardholders reach their annual renewal dates" ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/news/2026-credit-card-predictions/)); myFICO "Culling the herd" (~Aug 2026): "It's leaving less than 6 cents because of the AF" ([myFICO](https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Culling-the-herd/m-p/6871221)). We answer the dollar question (keep/downgrade/cancel); **suggestion #2** answers the effort question. Further idea: a renewal-month input so the verdict uses the fee the visitor will actually pay next (M, touches inputs).
2. **Credits that are hard to trigger or exclude people by date** (very common, freshest in the window). DoorDash stacking order and the $20 pickup minimum ([DoC, Oct 1/5](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-adding-doordash-15-monthly-credit-which-can-be-used-for-anything/)); CSR anniversary perks "only available to those who had the card as of Sept. 16, 2026" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-reserves-10th-anniversary-offer/)); the $300 dining credit's restaurant list "is not the same as the list of restaurants eligible for the $300 dining credit" ([AwardWallet](https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/sapphire-reserve-dining-credit/)); Amex Gold Reddit complaint via AOL that Uber and Grubhub "inflate delivery prices to account for any applied credits" ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/hidden-fees-coupon-cards-amex-222140506.html)). Ideas: show the friction tag text next to each credit (we have the badge; add "monthly, expires" / "specific merchant" wording), a one-line "delivery-app prices run higher" caveat on Uber/DoorDash credits, and an "existing cardholders only" flag so promotional credits never count for new applicants (S each).
3. **Nobody knows what a point is worth** (widespread among enthusiasts). Chase Points Boost: "flight bookings made with Chase points will likely be worth about 1 cent each, which is 20% to 33% less than before" and only "about 9.5% of the departure options had any Points Boost rate" ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/news/chase-points-boost-is-a-points-bust)); "the floor now around 1.15 cents per point" ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/chase-points-boost-is-becoming-a-points-bust-some-redemptions-now-only-1-15-cents-per-point/)); "Chase Travel portal: 5.94% more expensive than booking directly" ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/flights-travel-portals/)); "there are no official answers on what points and miles are worth" ([Upgraded Points, Oct 2026](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/points-and-miles-valuations/)). Our cash/transfer toggle covers the two ends; ideas: a "valuations as of {date}" stamp (S, runner-up above), a portal mark-up note on cards whose value depends on portal bookings (S, copy), and last week's per-card Hyatt caveat.
4. **Sign-up-bonus traps: eligibility pop-ups, rising spend requirements, "as high as" offers, clawbacks** (heavy 2026 coverage). Chase's per-card lifetime rule: "a missing pop-up means you're eligible for the bonus … but critically, it doesn't guarantee an approval" ([Thrifty Traveler](https://thriftytraveler.com/news/credit-card/chase-sapphire-eligibility-requirements/)); Amex Gold "on April 30, 2026, the spending requirement increased again from $6,000 to $8,000" ([Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-platinum-card-increases-spending-requirement/)) — note our Gold row says $6,000, another verifier item; "as high as" means "you cannot know your actual offer until you apply" ([TravelUpdate](https://travelupdate.com/is-this-the-future-of-amex-welcome-bonuses/)); refunds rescind a bonus ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/refunds-hurting-sign-up-bonus)); Citi's new application pop-up for the lifetime Strata rule ([DoC](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/citi-adds-pop-up-eligibility-for-new-lifetime-strata-rule/)). Our bonus check already says fees and refunds don't count; ideas: an "as high as" caveat line on Amex bonuses, "clock starts at approval, not card arrival", and a three-question eligibility pre-screen that greys out the bonus in the drawer (S to M).
5. **Application-rule confusion: 5/24, a reported 3/24 for sole proprietors, Capital One's unwritten limits** (evergreen; the 3/24 story is new and unconfirmed). "front line Chase reps are telling applicants that call reconsideration that there is a new rule for sole proprietors" with data points "mixed, with some 4/24 and 3/24 applicants approved and others denied" ([DoC, Sept 2026](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/has-chase-changed-the-5-24-rule-for-sole-props-3-24-for-sole-props/)); myFICO threads disagree on whether business cards count ([myFICO](https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Help-with-Chase-5-24-Mix-of-business-and-personal/td-p/6730216)).
