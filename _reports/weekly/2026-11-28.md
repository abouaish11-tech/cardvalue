## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Evidence is search snippets (about 20 queries); the search tool refuses `reddit.com`, so Reddit sentiment is second-hand via articles that quote r/CreditCards.

1. **"Is the annual fee worth it?" and coupon-book fatigue** (many; the dominant topic). Money.com Nov 5 on an r/CreditCards thread about cards kept only for perks ([Money](https://money.com/credit-cards-worth-keeping-for-perks/)); Reddit reaction to Robinhood's $695 card: "most of it comes in the form of niche monthly credits with restrictions, minimums, and timing rules" ([AOL/Motley Fool](https://www.aol.com/articles/heres-why-americans-switching-back-115040447.html)); myFICO: "(Not to mention the stress of using enough credits to cover that 3 digit AF!)" ([myFICO](https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/Evaluating-Annual-Fee-Cards/td-p/6602175)); Roaming Cactus on CSR: "if you forget about them, you're just lighting $795 on fire" ([Roaming Cactus](https://roamingcactus.com/credit-cards/csr-worth-it-2026)). **Idea:** the site already discounts credits by type and has the keep/downgrade verdict; the missing piece is per-credit "I'd use this" checkboxes feeding an effective fee (M). PR #7's effort-cost line is the S step.
2. **Nobody knows what their points are worth** (a few direct complaints, many explainers). Trustpilot reviewer: the app "should show the dollar value alongside the rewards" ([Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.capitalone.com?page=2)); FinanceBuzz: "there's no single way to calculate how many credit card points equal a dollar" ([FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/how-much-credit-card-points-worth)). **Already covered:** the drawer shows "75,000 bonus points ≈ $1,125 in value" and the cash/transfer toggle re-ranks consistently. No action.
3. **Category caps and what counts as "grocery"** (a few threads, many explainers). myFICO "AMEX Blue Cash Preferred Grocery Cap" ([myFICO](https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/AMEX-Blue-Cash-Preferred-Grocery-Cap/td-p/6809172)); Chase Q4 "excluding Walmart and Target", "Costco does not count as a grocery store" ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/news/chase-5-bonus-categories-q4-2026)); Sapphire Preferred's bonus is "online grocery purchases only" ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/grocerys-stores-vs-supermarkets-bonus-category/)). **Partly covered:** caps are applied in the maths and shown per category in the drawer; warehouse clubs are a separate spending input with an explainer. **Idea:** an "excludes Walmart/Target/Costco" badge next to grocery rates, which needs a per-card exclusions field from the verifier (S once the data exists).
4. **Missing the sign-up-bonus spend window** (a few). DoC commenter "was $60 short after 100 days and forgot about the card" ([Doctor of Credit](https://doctorofcredit.com/chase-enforcing-90-day-timeframe-for-signup-bonus-requirement)); "fees aren't purchases" ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/5-reasons-a-credit-card-company-wont-pay-out-your-sign-up-bonus)). **Covered on `main`** by the bonus check; PR #11's deadline planner adds the date side.
5. **Application rules (5/24 and friends) are opaque** (many). See suggestion #1. **Built this week.** Follow-ups: Amex once-per-lifetime and Citi's new lifetime Strata rule as one-line issuer tooltips (10-31 #3, still S).
6. **Holiday season: deferred-interest store cards, BNPL, carrying balances** (many surveys this month). NerdWallet survey: about one in four card users "expect they won't be able to pay in full and on time" ([Local 12](https://local12.com/news/nation-world/report-38-of-buy-now-pay-later-holiday-shoppers-expect-to-fall-short-on-payments-credit-cards)); "78% of store credit cards offering introductory zero-percent financing use deferred interest", APRs "rose to 33.14% in October" ([SuperMoney](https://www.supermoney.com/store-card-black-friday-deferred-interest), [Adalytica](https://adalytica.com/news/store-credit-cards-holiday-financing-costs-c9318ffb)); 27% "regret signing up for at least one" ([FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/retail-credit-card-survey)). **Idea:** PR #10's carried-balance panel is the general answer; a "deferred interest" flag on our five store cards is the seasonal one (runner-up above).
7. **Too many cards / downgrade vs cancel** (many, long-running). Bogleheads "Credit card rewards vs hassle factor" ([Bogleheads](https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=408767)); "the difference between a 1.5% and 2% cash back card is $500" at $100K ([Bogleheads](https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=470880)); CNN "Don't cancel… Downgrade them instead" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/money/do-not-cancel-credit-cards-with-annual-fee)). **Covered:** the wallet builder's 1/2/3/4-card modes show the marginal gain of complexity (+$949 → +$1,415 → +$1,753 → +$1,980 at default spending) and the keep/downgrade verdict names the downgrade target. No action.
8. **Statement credits don't post reliably** (a few notable reviews). Trustpilot: "about the 5th time that AMEX has not credited me" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.americanexpress.com)); ConsumerAffairs: $300 hotel credit never posted ([ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/travel/amex_travel.html)); TPG: Resy credits took 10 weeks ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/amex-platinum-use-credits-before-end-quarter)). **Idea:** "enrollment required" marker per credit (S, needs a data field); PR #7's effort-cost line is adjacent.

Weakly evidenced this month: foreign-transaction-fee surprises (generic explainers only); a "No FTF" filter chip remains a trivial S if wanted.
