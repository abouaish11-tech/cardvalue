## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Source: the "pain" sub-agent (40 searches, snippets only; Reddit threads are not indexable from the sandbox, so Reddit evidence is second-hand via blogs that summarise r/CreditCards posts; few items could be dated inside Sept 10 – Oct 10, dates are approximate). Ranked by apparent frequency.

1. **"Is the annual fee worth it?" and the retention trap** (frequent). "69,500 points to wipe out the old $695 fee, with no alternate offers on the table" ([TravelUpdate 2026](https://travelupdate.com/amex-platinum-card-best-retention-offer-bonus-2026/)); "a 10% rate on spending you do not do, paid in a currency you cannot easily use, behind a $695 fee, can lose to a boring 2% card" ([Card-Savvy](https://card-savvy.com/learn/most-exciting-credit-card-launches-2026)); 57% say no annual fee matters ([NerdWallet/Harris, Apr 2026](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/studies/how-many-credit-cards)). **We now answer this** in the drawer (keep/downgrade/cancel + break-even). Next: a "which credits would you actually use?" tick-list that re-scores credits (M, touches ranking inputs, owner sign-off).
2. **Coupon-book credits that go unused** (frequent). A credit "slipped their mind until too late… lost out on over $80" ([YMMV, Mar 24 2026](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/03/24/amex-platinum-card-worth-it-1815-value/)); "keeping track of overlapping credits across multiple AMEX cards can get complicated fast" ([YMMV, Jan 7 2026](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/01/07/amex-platinum-credits-first-week-550/)); United TravelBank no longer triggers the airline credit since Feb 5 ([Loyalty Lobby](https://loyaltylobby.com/2026/01/10/american-express-annual-travel-credits-have-reset-use-your-2026-funds-timely/)). We already discount credits by type and show face vs realistic. Idea: a per-credit "cadence / enrollment required / effort" column in the drawer's credit list and an optional calendar (.ics) of reset dates (M).
3. **Not knowing what points are worth; portal-only value** (frequent among enthusiasts). Points Boost "now only 1.15 cents per point" ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/chase-points-boost-is-becoming-a-points-bust-some-redemptions-now-only-1-15-cents-per-point/)); fewer than 10% of portal flights qualified ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/learn/how-to-maximize-chase-ultimate-rewards)); "once booked via portal on American and it was a disaster" ([Bogleheads](https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=460771)); 48% say programs are too complicated ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/studies/how-many-credit-cards)). Idea: suggestion #3 above, plus showing every points figure with its cash floor ("60,000 pts ≈ $900 transfer / $600 cash") (S).
4. **Category caps, exclusions and merchant coding** (moderate-to-frequent). "WNM, Target, etc will not count under the grocery category" ([FlyerTalk Custom Cash thread](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/citi-thankyou-rewards/2042222-new-citi-icustom-cashn-card-5-top-eligible-category-5.html)); BCP 6% "capped at $6,000… excludes wholesale clubs, superstores and online grocery retailers" ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/reviews/is-blue-cash-preferred-worth-it)). We show cap warnings in the drawer; exclusions need a per-card `notableExclusions` field and the "where do you buy groceries?" toggle from the 09-20 report (M).
5. **Autopay failures and surprise interest** (moderate). Amex autopay failed Aug 23 for some, "late fees were assessed… Membership Rewards points… were forfeited" ([Danny the Deal Guru](https://dannydealguru.com/amex-autopay-failure-late-fees-points/), [Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/amex-autopay-issues-for-some-customers/)); "it's easy to pick [minimum payment] by accident" ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/dont-use-auto-pay-until-you-check-this-credit-card-setting)). Out of our ranking scope; a one-paragraph "set autopay to statement balance" note in the footer/methodology would be cheap (S, copy).
6. **Downgrade vs cancel and fee-refund windows** (moderate, in-window). Refund "when a card is closed within 60 days of the fee posting date"; a Citi downgrade "came with a 51-day window"; another "still got charged a $495 AF and was told to wait 8–12 weeks" ([OMAAT comments, late Sept](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/credit-card-annual-fee-refund-rules/)); "downgrading or canceling may affect your eligibility for future welcome bonuses" ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/guide/cancel-or-downgrade-credit-card)). **Now addressed** by the keep/downgrade box (it names the target and says to confirm with the issuer). Next: per-issuer refund-window days in the box's footnote (S, needs a small issuer table).
7. **Too many cards, want to simplify** (frequent). "Spending the time and energy to manage seven cards simply isn't worth it for most people" ([AOL on r/CreditCards](https://aol.com/7-cards-credit-card-game-180411088.html)); 33% say they have too many ([NerdWallet/Harris](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/studies/how-many-credit-cards)). Idea: "cards I already hold" input → marginal value of each, which to sock-drawer (M/L, a second optimizer; owner design).
8. **Missing the sign-up-bonus spend window** (moderate; direct Reddit evidence is from Dec 2025). A Chase cardholder "was told on the phone they had missed the deadline, but the issuer later apologized" ([r/churning snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1peehbk/snapshots/2025-12-05T06%3A30%3A56.790066Z)); "you don't want to reach day 89 with another $200 to go" ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/5-reasons-a-credit-card-company-wont-pay-out-your-sign-up-bonus)). **Now addressed** by the bonus check. Next: a "bonus achievable at my spend" filter chip (S).

Themes with insufficient evidence this month: 5/24 (guides only, no 2026 posts), foreign transaction fees (one Fodor's anecdote; a "no FX fee" chip/filter would still be cheap once the data has a boolean), trailing interest (explainers only), issuer app reviews (stores unreachable).
