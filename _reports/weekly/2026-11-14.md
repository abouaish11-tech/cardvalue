### 1. "Do you carry a balance?" interest-versus-rewards reality check — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** New; not in any of the seven prior reports (checked for APR, interest and pay-in-full). Pain theme 8 is the one with population-scale evidence rather than enthusiast anecdotes: the New York Fed's Q3 report (Nov 10) has card balances at a record $1.29 trillion ([Axios](https://axios.com/2026/11/10/credit-card-debt-record-2026-delinquency-income-gap), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/credit-card-debt-record.html)); the Fed's G.19 release of Nov 6 puts the average rate on accounts assessed interest at 22.36% ([federalreserve.gov](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/20261106/g19.pdf)); a 2026 survey has 22% of cardholders not knowing their APR ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/2026-survey-as-inflation-pressures-persist-americans-are-increasingly-relying-on-credit-cards-to-bridge-the-gap-302713862.html)); Debt.com's 2026 survey has the share with $10K+ in card debt up from 22% to 29% ([Debt.com](https://www.debt.com/research/credit-card-survey/)); the buzz agent found a Dave Ramsey "can't afford my credit card payments" clip at 56× its usual views on Nov 8 and the Warren/Trump 10% APR-cap story running all week ([Reason, Nov 13](https://reason.com/2026/11/13/capping-credit-card-interest-an-idea-so-bad-that-both-democrats-and-republicans-support-it/)). A rewards site that never mentions interest is answering the wrong question for a third of its visitors.
- **What:** optional `<details>` "Do you carry a balance?" in the spending drawer with two labelled inputs (balance carried, APR with the 22.4% default and its source); `renderInterestNote(net)` under the stage readout: "Carrying $3,000 at 22.4% costs about $672 a year in interest, which takes this card's +$934 down to +$262. Paying it down is worth more than most card switches." Three variants (interest exceeds the card's net; interest smaller than net; card already negative). Follows the carousel, saved in `localStorage` (`cv_balance`), cleared by Reset.
- **Effort:** S (built in about an hour). **Impact:** trust, and the honest answer for the visitor who should not be shopping for a rewards card at all; also a differentiator every comparison site avoids because it suppresses applications. **Must not touch:** ranking, wallet maths, paywall, checkout, licence logic, any card value. It doesn't; `rankingUnchanged: true` in the targeted test, and `AVG_CARD_APR` is a display constant only.

FlyerTalk's Chase product-change thread is 31+ pages with conflicting refund rules ([thread](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/chase-ultimate-rewards/2113574-chase-product-change-card-conversion-downgrade-upgrade-keep-cancel-2023-26-a-31.html)); myFICO "Amex Platinum - Downgrade or Cancel?"
