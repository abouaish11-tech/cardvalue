### 2. Bonus deadline planner in the drawer — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** New; no prior report proposed it (10-31 #3 was a repeat-bonus checkbox and 11-14 #2 was issuer-side offer end dates; this is the applicant's side of the same problem). Pain theme 7 this week: "the minimum-spend clock starts ticking from the day you're approved, not from the day you receive the card" ([point.me](https://www.point.me/c/insights/how-to-leverage-your-holiday-shopping-to-earn-points-for-a-free-flight)); purchases "just before a spending deadline might not post until after it has passed" ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/do-this-1-thing-to-avoid-missing-out-on-a-credit-card-bonus)); the 11-14 report's FlyerTalk thread of a bonus missed by $17 and the CFPB's "unexpected promotional conditions" as complaint type #1. The buzz side is all dated offers people are applying for this fortnight: Southwest by Dec 2 ([TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/news/last-chance-earn-southwest-companion-pass-with-card-bonuses/)), Apple Card by Dec 10 ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/18/apple-card-double-daily-cash/)), Hilton Aspire's 200K by Jan 13 ([InsideFlyer](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/hilton-aspire-offer-pairs-200-000-points-with-a-free-night/)), the Venture 75K + $300 with no announced end ([OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/deals/capital-one-venture-75k-miles)). Holiday spending is when people open a card to hit a bonus, and the window maths is the part they get wrong.
- **What:** `renderBonusPlanner(bonus)` under `renderBonusCheck` in the drawer's sign-up bonus section: a labelled `<input type="date">` defaulting to today, the window-close date (`addMonths`, with a last-day-of-month guard so Jan 31 + 1 month is Feb 28, not Mar 3), a spend-by date 10 days earlier (`BONUS_POSTING_BUFFER_DAYS`), and a note that the clock starts at approval and some issuers count 90 days. The date persists while the visitor browses other cards (`bonusApprovedOn`), and resets to today if cleared.
- **Effort:** S (about an hour). **Impact:** trust and usefulness on the free rows (the drawer is open to ranks 4–5 unlicensed), and it pairs with 11-14 #2 if the verifier ever adds `signupBonus.ends`. **Must not touch:** ranking, wallet, paywall, checkout, licence logic, any card value. It doesn't; `rankingUnchanged: true` in the targeted test at both widths.

## Customer pain themes and product ideas

Pain agent, about 33 searches. **Reddit was unreachable even as a domain filter**, issuer-app store reviews surfaced nothing rewards-specific, CFPB rewards complaint data returned nothing, and myFICO/Bogleheads returned only 2019–2024 threads. Where a quote's date is not visible it is said so.

1. **Coupon-book fatigue** (very common). Frequent Miler reader: "the coupon book part of it is so annoying but esp when they don't credit the coupon for weeks on end" ([link](https://frequentmiler.com/is-the-795-sapphire-reserve-coupon-card-a-keeper/), undated); Max Miles Points on the Oct 1 DoorDash restructuring: "The Chase Sapphire Reserve coupon book is frustrating and requires more steps to redeem" ([link](https://www.maxmilespoints.com/blog/chase-sapphire-reserve-doordash-credit-mess-here-s-just-changed)); TravelUpdate: back-to-back stays at the same hotel now count as one stay for The Edit credit ([link](https://travelupdate.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-gets-more-coupon-book-treatment-with-new-restriction/)); "Don't Lose $1,000+: Amex Platinum Q4 Credits Expire Dec 31" ([Kudos](https://www.joinkudos.com/blog/dont-waste-1-000-how-to-use-your-q4-amex-platinum-credits-before-they-expire)). *We have:* realization discounts by credit type. *Idea:* the one-line effort cost already built in unmerged PR #7; a Dec-31 badge on calendar-year credits (S).
2. **"Is the annual fee still worth it?"** (very common). Fortune, Mar 30: Chase "raised the Sapphire Reserve annual fee from $450 to $795 while reducing the redemption value of certain rewards points" ([link](https://fortune.com/2026/03/30/credit-card-annual-fees-soaring-why-people-keep-paying-them-perks-robinhood-american-express-chase/)); Money.com, Nov 5, summarising r/CreditCards on which cards people keep for the perks ([link](https://money.com/credit-cards-worth-keeping-for-perks/)); Forbes' Reddit roundup: the Strata Premier hotel credit "can be hard to use" ([link](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/credit-cards/best-credit-cards-consumer-sentiment-reddit-lmandp5/)). *We have:* the keep/downgrade/cancel verdict, which was rendering unstyled until today's fix. *Idea:* tick-the-credits-you'd-use recompute (M, repeat from 11-14).
3. **Nobody knows what a point is worth** (high among enthusiasts). "all Chase cardholders now get the same 1 cent per point value in Chase Travel" outside Boost fares ([Points Playbook](https://www.pointsplaybook.co/blog/chase-travel-portal-complete-guide-2026/)); "Chase hasn't published a floor for Points Boost values" ([Thrifty Traveler](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/chase-points-boost-guide/)); Marriott fell "from 0.8 cents per point in 2025 to 0.7 cent per point in 2026" ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/how-points-and-miles-values-changed-in-2026)). *We have:* the Cash / Transfer toggle. *Idea:* print the assumed ¢/pt inline on every points card's value (S, repeat).
4. **Holiday debt: interest wipes out rewards** (very common). "38% of Americans said they are still paying off something they bought last holiday season" (College Investor, n=600, [link](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/91817/holiday-debt-survey-2026/)); "two in three Americans with unsecured debt (66%) are still paying off last year's holiday spending" (National Debt Relief/Censuswide, Nov 18, [link](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/two-in-three-americans-in-debt-still-paying-for-last-years-holiday-cheer-302942889.html)); LendingTree average new-offer APR "23.96%, up from 23.82% in September" ([link](https://www.lendingtree.com/credit-cards/study/average-credit-card-interest-rate-in-america/)). *Built* in unmerged PR #10 (the carried-balance reality check).
5. **Store cards at checkout; deferred interest is not 0% APR** (seasonal spike). WalletHub, data collected Nov 10: "the average deferred interest transparency score remained unchanged in 2026" ([link](https://wallethub.com/edu/cc/deferred-interest-study/25707)); Bankrate: "if you owe even a penny on the balance after the introductory period expires, you may owe up to 100 percent of the interest costs accrued" ([link](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/zero-interest/zero-percent-intro-apr-guide/)); LendingTree: average new store-card APR "30.58 percent". *We list* Target Circle, Target Mastercard, Sam's Club, Amazon Prime Visa, OnePay. *Idea:* a short "deferred interest vs true 0%" explainer and a tag on store cards (S; parked as off-core).
6. **BNPL vs card** (moderate-high this season). "38% of holiday shoppers expect to use buy now, pay later" (NerdWallet/Harris via [Money Under 30](https://www.moneyunder30.com/holiday-budget-2026-avoid-debt/)); BNPL borrowers "much more likely than non-users to carry credit card balances from month to month" ([24/7 Wall St, Nov 4](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/04/americans-are-turning-to-buy-now-pay-later-for-the-holidays-and-trouble-paying-is-already-brewing/)); sources contradict each other on whether Pay-in-4 hits FICO. *Idea:* a BNPL-vs-card table (S; off-core, parked).
7. **Application rules and bonus windows** (high in churning circles). "Chase might have a new 3/24 for business cards for sole proprietors (but possibly only Ink cards)" ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/chase-might-have-a-new-3-24-for-business-cards-for-sole-proprietors-but-possibly-only-ink-cards/)); one tally: "6 of 45 Chase Ink applications from sole props at 3+/24 were approved" ([Katie's Travel Tricks](https://katiestraveltricks.com/chase324rule/)); "The minimum-spend clock starts ticking from the day you're approved, not from the day you receive the card" ([point.me](https://www.point.me/c/insights/how-to-leverage-your-holiday-shopping-to-earn-points-for-a-free-flight)); purchases "just before a spending deadline might not post until after it has passed" ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/do-this-1-thing-to-avoid-missing-out-on-a-credit-card-bonus)). *Built this week:* the bonus deadline planner. *Still parked:* the 5/24 input (09-26, 10-10; enthusiast evidence only).
8. **Fine-print gotchas: activation, caps, FX fees, network** (steady). "You have to 'activate' the categories each time"; Discover earns from activation day, Chase retroactively; Citi Dividend's "$6,000 annual cap rather than a $1,500 quarterly cap" ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/q4-2026-5-quarterly-categories-activate-offers-suggestions-freedom-flex-discover-dividend-cash-more/)); "roughly a third of Americans do not know whether their credit card charges a foreign transaction fee" ([WalletHub](https://wallethub.com/edu/cc/foreign-transaction-fees/19571)); new Venture/Savor/Quicksilver on the Discover network, "occasionally you won't be able to use it, especially overseas" ([Clark Howard via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/currencies/articles/won-t-clark-howard-warns-164159288.html)). *Idea:* "Needs quarterly activation" tag and inline cap text (S, runner-up); FX-fee and network fields (verifier).

Lower-signal (older evidence only): "too many cards / simplify" and "downgrade vs cancel". A one-line "Downgrade, don't cancel" tip under the cancel verdict is S and safe.
