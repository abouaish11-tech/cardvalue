## Top 3 suggestions

### 1. Dated "Announced change" notice on cards with a known future change — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** New this week; not in a prior report (the 10-24 runner-up "changed in the last 90 days" dot was about *past* changes). This week's four biggest stories are all effective in 2027, so every number we show for those cards is right today and wrong in two to twelve weeks: Citi AAdvantage Platinum Select fee $99 → $150 on Jan 24, 2027 ([CNBC, Oct 27](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/27/american-airlines-citi-aadvantage-card-fee.html); [Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/citi-aadvantage-platinum-select-card-revamp-coming-january-2027-150-annual-fee-new-benefits/); [TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/news/citi-aadvantage-platinum-select-business-refresh)); Amex Platinum's hotel credit needs two-night stays from Jan 1, 2027 ([View from the Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/amex-confirms-platinums-300-hotel-credit-will-require-2-nights-starting-january-1-adds-airbnb-luxe-option/); [Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/american-express-fine-hotels-resorts-credit-to-require-two-night-stay-will-include-airbnb-luxe-properties/); [Eye of the Flyer, Oct 22](https://eyeoftheflyer.com/2026/10/22/amex-platinum-lodging-credit-two-night-minimum/)); Bilt's 2027 overhaul ([Doctor of Credit, Oct 27](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/bilt-revamps-credit-card-rewards-in-2027-discontinuing-4-bilt-cash-for-new-applicants-more/); [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/bilt-rewards-program-changes-2027/); [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/news/bilt-program-changes-2027-bilt-cash-credit-cards-hotel-redemptions/)). The pain research's top theme (fee creep, ~15 sources) proposes exactly this: show increases "announced but not yet in force" with the effective date, e.g. "+$51 from Jan 2027". A visitor comparing the AA Platinum Select today sees a $99 fee and a positive verdict that flips in January.
- **What:** `upcoming: { date, text, source }` (one or a list) per card. Drawer: amber "Announced change, effective January 24, 2027" block with the text, the sentence "Every number on this page describes the card as it is today", and a Source link. Table row and stage: "· changes Jan 24, 2027" after the issuer. Wallet-builder card: small chip. Compare: "Announced changes" row when any compared card has one. Rendered from the existing amber tokens, same family as the closed-to-applicants state.
- **Effort:** S (built in about an hour). **Impact:** trust with the audience that reads the news first, and fewer "your fee is wrong" emails in January. **Must not touch:** ranking, `pointsValuations`, wallet maths, paywall, checkout. It doesn't.

### 2. Amex Membership Rewards "not pooled" footnote in the keep/downgrade verdict and the wallet builder (S)
- **Repeat** of 10-24 #2 (and 10-17 #3), stated as such; still unbuilt because the owner was asked to confirm the per-issuer wording. New evidence this week: Frequent Miler, Oct 26, "Amex no longer automatically linking Membership Rewards accounts" ([snippet](https://frequentmiler.com/amex-no-longer-automatically-linking-membership-rewards-accounts/)); Upgraded Points frames it as a trap, points in an unlinked account are forfeited when its last card is cancelled ([snippet](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-changes-points-pooling/)); Your Mileage May Vary's Oct 16 "is everyone overreacting?" concludes it is fine *as long as you link* ([YMMV](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/10/16/amex-membership-rewards-changes-overreaction/)). Pain theme 3 (downgrade vs cancel, ~9 sources) is the same risk from the visitor's side. Four of the ten most-discussed cards this week earn Membership Rewards.
- **What:** `CANCEL_NOTES` keyed by `currency`, one footnote line under the verdict when it is Downgrade or Cancel; for `amex_mr`: "Cards opened since Oct 2026 keep their points in a separate account until you link them under Manage Connections. Link before you ever close one." One line under the wallet summary when two or more wallet cards share `amex_mr`. The Amex sentence is sourced above; Chase, Citi and Capital One wording still needs the owner to confirm against the issuers' terms.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust; prevents the one mistake that turns a sensible downgrade into forfeiting a bonus' worth of points. **Must not touch:** verdict maths, `DOWNGRADE_PATHS`, optimizer, paywall.

### 3. "I've had this card (or its family) before" checkbox in the bonus check, with the issuer's repeat-bonus rule in one line (S)
- **Why now:** New this week as a top-3 (5/24 was proposed on 09-26; this is the once-per-lifetime side). Pain theme 7 (~10 sources): Citi replaced its 48-month rule with once-per-lifetime for Strata cards, visible on Citi pages as of Sept 23, 2026 with a pre-application pop-up ([US Credit Card Guide, snippet](https://www.uscreditcardguide.com/best-credit-card-sign-up-bonus/)); Chase ended the 48-month/"one Sapphire" rules in favour of per-product limits ([AskSebby, snippet](https://www.asksebby.com/articles/capital-one-duo-venture-x-savor-strategy); [Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/memo-chase-changing-rules-around-signup-bonus-eligibility-for-sapphire-reserve-sapphire-preferred/)); Capital One's Venture X bonus is off the table if you got one on the same product in the past 48 months ([One Mile at a Time, updated Oct 3](https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/capital-one/capital-one-venture-x-card/)). Our drawer already does "can you hit the bonus?" against the visitor's spend; it says nothing about whether they can *get* it at all, and this is a bonus-spike week (United 100K, Southwest 90K, Delta to Nov 4).
- **What:** one checkbox in the drawer's bonus section ("I've held this card, or one in its family, before"); when ticked, the bonus line is struck through with "Probably not eligible: {issuer rule}" from a small `BONUS_RULES` table keyed by issuer (Amex: once per lifetime per card; Citi Strata: once per lifetime; Chase: per-product, 24 or 48 months by card; Capital One: 48 months per product). Display only; the sign-up bonus is not part of net value today, so nothing in the ranking moves. Rule wording should be confirmed against issuer terms before shipping (the links above are blogs).
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust, and fewer wasted applications. **Must not touch:** ranking, paywall, wallet maths.
