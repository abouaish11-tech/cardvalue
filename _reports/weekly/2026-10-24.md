## Top 3 suggestions

### 1. One-line "effort cost" above each card's statement credits — BUILT (S)
- **Why now:** Repeat of last week's #2, stated as such; it was marked "ready to build next week" and the week's evidence is all about credit *effort*, not face value. The Platinum's Oct 22 changes add an up-to-$250 Airbnb Experiences credit that only unlocks after an Airbnb Luxe booking, which TravelUpdate calls "one of the most complicated benefits", and from Jan 1 2027 the hotel credit needs two-night stays ([TPG, Oct 22](https://thepointsguy.com/news/amex-platinum-airbnb-hotel-credit-changes/); [TravelUpdate](https://travelupdate.com/amex-platinum-card-changes-2026-updated/); [DoC](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/american-express-fine-hotels-resorts-credit-to-require-two-night-stay-will-include-airbnb-luxe-properties/)). Business Platinum's wireless credit and 2x shipping narrow to named providers from Feb 17 2027 ([Frequent Miler, Oct 23](https://frequentmiler.com/amex-business-platinum-changes-zo-workspace-memberhip-narrowed-wireless-credits-and-shipping-category/)). Pain theme 2 is the "coupon book" again: a Slickdeals thread relaying Reddit says the Platinum "has been turned into a coupon book" ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/17480091-multiple-amex-platinum-offers-for-40th-anniversary)), and eMarketer reports that "steep annual fees, statement credit rules risk alienating credit cardholders" ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/steep-annual-fees--statement-credit-rules-risk-alienating-credit-cardholders)). Cardstack's estimate that an engaged Platinum holder extracts roughly $1,500–2,200 and a passive one about $500 ([Cardstack](https://cardstack.money/articles/credit-card-reviews/amex-platinum-effective-annual-fee-2026)) is exactly the gap this line names.
- **What:** `creditEffortSummary(credits)` counts `drip`, `portal` and `coupon` credits as needing action and `automatic` and `flexible` as hands-off, and renders one sentence above the credit list (amber), or a green "Nothing to track" variant when every credit posts on its own. The compare modal's Realistic Credits row shows "10 of 10 need action" under the dollar figure.
- **Effort:** S (built in about an hour). **Impact:** trust with the visitors deciding whether to keep an $895/$795 card; it answers "is the fee worth it" in the unit people argue in. **Must not touch:** `CREDIT_REALIZATION` weights, ranking, wallet maths, paywall. It doesn't.

### 2. Amex Membership Rewards "not pooled" warning in the drawer, the keep/downgrade verdict and the wallet builder (S)
- **Why now:** New rule this month: since about Oct 15, "new cards earning Membership Rewards points will by default be placed in a separate Membership Rewards account" ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/american-express-no-longer-automatically-pools-membership-rewards-points-from-new-cards/)); "this seems to only apply to new cards, and not to existing cards, at least for the time being" ([One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/news/amex-stops-automatically-pooling-membership-rewards-points-new-cards/)); View from the Wing's headline is "check this before you cancel a card" ([VFTW](https://viewfromthewing.com/amex-stops-automatically-pooling-new-card-points-check-this-before-you-cancel-a-card/)); YMMV asks whether "everyone is overreacting" and concludes it is fine as long as you link ([YMMV, Oct 16](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/10/16/amex-membership-rewards-changes-overreaction/)). This merges last week's #3 (what happens to your points if you cancel) with its runner-up (a wallet-builder note when it picks two MR cards); the default wallet today is Platinum + Freedom Flex, and any 3- or 4-card wallet with Gold or Blue Business Plus alongside Platinum is exactly the case.
- **What:** a `CANCEL_NOTES` table keyed by `currency`, rendered as one footnote under the verdict when it is Downgrade or Cancel; for `amex_mr` the sentence "Cards opened since Oct 2026 keep their points in a separate account until you link them under Manage Connections. Link before you ever close one." Also one line under the wallet summary when two or more wallet cards share `amex_mr`. Per-issuer wording for Chase, Citi and Capital One should be confirmed by the owner against the issuers' terms before shipping (the sources above cover Amex).
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** trust; prevents the one mistake that turns a sensible downgrade into forfeiting a sign-up bonus' worth of points. **Must not touch:** the verdict maths, `DOWNGRADE_PATHS`, the optimizer, paywall.

## Customer pain themes and product ideas

From the pain agent (39 searches; Reddit, FlyerTalk, MyFICO, Bogleheads, Quora and app stores returned nothing, so quotes come from blog comment sections and consumer press exposed in snippets):

1. **"The fee went up again, is it still worth it, do I downgrade or cancel?"** (most common; spiking as the first $795 Sapphire Reserve renewals land around Oct 26 and IHG/United notices arrive). "There is no grandfather clause; every renewing cardholder gets the new fee and the new credit stack" ([creditpoints.cash](https://creditpoints.cash/news/amex-platinum-895-one-year-later-2026)); "make sure you call Chase to downgrade within 30 days to avoid owing a prorated amount" ([US News](https://money.usnews.com/credit-cards/articles/your-chase-sapphire-reserve-fee-is-shooting-up-heres-how-to-downgrade)); Fortune's "fee creep" piece ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/premium-credit-cards-hiked-annual-110000639.html)). **Ideas:** a renewal-window helper in the drawer (fee-post month → 30-day downgrade deadline, S); a fee-history mini-timeline per card (S with 3–4 data points); a downgrade-path "what you lose / what you keep" map (M). We already have the verdict.
2. **The coupon book: credits that expire monthly and get forgotten** (very common; the December use-it-or-lose-it crunch is six weeks away). Platinum "has been turned into a coupon book" ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/17480091-multiple-amex-platinum-offers-for-40th-anniversary)); issuers rely on breakage ([Card-Savvy](https://card-savvy.com/learn/premium-card-credits-coupon-book-2026)). **Built this week** as the effort line. Further ideas: a per-card credit checklist with "I'd actually use this" (M); a breakage slider (S once cadence data exists); a Nov/Dec "resets Jan 1, unused value $Z" nudge (S).
3. **"What are my points even worth now?"** (very common; the Hyatt 4:3 for Chase Preferred/Ink holders is live since Oct 1 and Bilt's Dec 31 deadline is new). "Chase Points Boost is becoming a Points Bust" ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/chase-points-boost-is-becoming-a-points-bust-some-redemptions-now-only-1-15-cents-per-point/)); valuations diverge (TPG 2.05¢ for Chase UR vs OMAAT 1.7¢, [TPG](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/monthly-valuations/), [OMAAT](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/value-miles-points/)); "45.1% of travel card holders have let their points or miles expire unused" ([BestMoney survey](https://www.bestmoney.com/credit-cards/learn-more/travel-credit-card-rewards-survey)). **Ideas:** a transfer-ratio note on Chase/Bilt cards (S, data + badge); a "changed in the last 90 days" badge (S UI); a "beats 2% cash only if you redeem at ≥ X¢/pt" line (S); a redemption-style selector replacing the binary toggle (M).
4. **NEW: Amex no longer auto-pools Membership Rewards on new cards** (new this month; churner/multi-card concern). Sources in suggestion #2. **Ideas:** the warning strip and verdict footnote in suggestion #2 (S).
5. **Sign-up-bonus mechanics: rising spend windows and "approved but no bonus"** (common, evergreen; thresholds and rule rewrites are 2026 developments; holiday timing is seasonal now). Platinum requirement $8K → $12K ([US Credit Card Guide](https://www.uscreditcardguide.com/amex-platinum-charge-card/)); Chase Sapphire eligibility "can also depend on the number of cards you have opened and closed" ([TPG](https://www.thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026/)); refunds near the deadline can leave you short ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/surprising-way-might-lose-credit-131518716.html)). **Ideas:** holiday-timing sentence in the bonus check (S, suggestion #3b); "returns reduce qualifying spend, keep a 5–10% buffer" copy (S); an eligibility pre-check panel that zeroes the bonus for ineligible cards (M).
6. **Q4 rotating categories: activation rules differ, caps are small, Discover lives inside Capital One now** (seasonal, moderate). Sources in suggestion #3. Of 89 negative Play Store reviews of the migrated Discover app, "23 are about a balance the app no longer shows" ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/discover-card-moved-to-capital-one-app-reviews-2026)). **Ideas:** suggestion #3a; "now serviced by Capital One; activate under Rewards & Benefits" copy on Discover cards (S).
7. **Category coding: "my grocery card gave me 1% at Walmart/Target/Costco"** (very common, evergreen). Issuers key off merchant codes and exclude superstores ([CardRatings](https://www.cardratings.com/rewards/how-do-credit-card-issuers-define-grocery-purchases.html)); "6% cash back on up to $6,000" ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/best/groceries)). We already split warehouse clubs from supermarkets. **Ideas:** a cap indicator in the drawer ("first $6,000 at 6%, the remaining $X at 1%", S if caps are in the data, which they are for most capped cards).
8. **Quiet benefit cuts and surprise fees on set-and-forget cards** (moderate volume, many incidents; IHG/United letters make it current). Freedom Flex cell-phone protection gone Sept 20 with "no public announcement" ([InsideFlyer](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/chase-ends-cell-phone-protection-on-freedom-flex/)); Venture X guests "$45 per visit" ([CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/capital-one-venture-x-vs-citi-strata-elite/)); Bilt 2.0 "dozens of complaints from current cardholders about being rejected" ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/bilt-2-0-transition-not-seamless-for-all-cardholders/)). **Ideas:** a per-card "recent changes" accordion fed by a small array (S UI, M backfill); FTF warning and "no FTF only" chip (S, needs a data field); protection filters (M).

Honourable mentions: closing cards and credit-score utilisation (a one-line utilisation estimate in the cancel branch, S); holiday debt, 26% still carrying last year's ([CardRates](https://www.cardrates.com/studies/survey-holiday-credit-card-debt/)), which argues for an "if you carry a balance, rewards don't matter" switch (S).

New this month vs evergreen: Amex MR non-pooling, Hyatt 4:3 live for Chase and Bilt's Dec 31 deadline, IHG/United fee letters, Q4 categories inside the Capital One app, the first $795 Reserve renewals, holiday bonus timing are new; fee-worth-it, coupon book, point values, bonus windows, category coding and FTF are evergreen.
