## Modelling approximations

- Shared caps across two categories (Southwest Plus/Premier, Discover it Chrome, Gemini) are split per category.

- Amex Business Platinum's 2x business categories and Business Gold's advertising and software categories have no matching spending category, so they earn the base rate here.
- Several Amex and Delta welcome offers are limited-time (most end 11/4/2026); Friday's recheck will update them.
